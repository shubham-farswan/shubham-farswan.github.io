Beyond the Lab

Outside of physics, I like designing and building things end-to-end — from circuits to firmware to apps. A running instrument in the lab is often as much electronics and code as it is physics.

What I build

Electronics

Analog and digital circuit design, PCB layout, sensors, and instrumentation for measurement and control.

Programming

Data acquisition, analysis, and automation — Python for scientific computing, plus general-purpose scripting and tooling.

App Development

Building desktop, and web application — for lab utilities.

Embedded Systems

Microcontroller firmware (Arduino, ESP32, STM32), real-time control, and hardware–software integration.
