About

I am a fourth-year PhD research scholar in the Department of Physics at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Delhi, advised by Dr. Kaushik Sen. My research combines thin-film growth by pulsed laser deposition (PLD) with low-temperature Raman spectroscopy, growing quantum materials and then probing their lattice, spin, and electronic excitations.

I am especially interested in multiferroics - where magnetism and ferroelectricity coexist and couple and in quantum spin liquid candidates, whose exotic magnetic ground states leave subtle fingerprints in their Raman response. Alongside physics, I enjoy building things: electronics, software, and embedded systems (see Beyond the Lab).

  • Advisor: Dr. Kaushik Sen
  • Institute: IIT Delhi
  • Methods: PLD thin-film growth & low-temperature Raman
  • Systems: Multiferroics, quantum spin liquids
