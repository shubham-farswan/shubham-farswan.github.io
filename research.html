Research

I grow quantum-material thin films by pulsed laser deposition and study them with temperature and polarization-dependent Raman scattering, down to cryogenic temperatures - tracking phonons, magnons, and their coupling across phase transitions.

Current directions

Multiferroics

Spin–phonon and magnetoelectric coupling probed through phonon anomalies, symmetry breaking, and mode softening across magnetic and ferroelectric transitions.

Quantum spin liquid candidates

Searching for signatures of fractionalized and continuum excitations in the magnetic Raman response of frustrated magnets.

Low-temperature Raman spectroscopy

Cryogenic, polarization-resolved measurements to disentangle lattice, spin, and electronic contributions to the scattered light.

Thin-film growth (PLD)

Synthesizing high-quality thin films of quantum materials by pulsed laser deposition, enabling controlled, sample-tailored spectroscopy.

Techniques & methods

  • Growth: Pulsed laser deposition (PLD) of thin films
  • Spectroscopy: Raman scattering (temperature- & polarization-dependent)
  • Environment: Cryogenic / low-temperature setups
  • Analysis: Phonon & magnon mode fitting, spin–phonon coupling
  • Systems: Multiferroics, frustrated magnets, QSL candidates
